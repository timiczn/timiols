## Project Highlights

[Nexus-Care-Athlete-Injury-Intelligence](https://github.com/timiczn/Nexus-Care-Athlete-Injury-Intelligence/blob/main/README.md)

Nexus Care is the medical and performance unit responsible for managing athlete health across multiple sports clubs, events, and regions. Despite logging thousands of injury cases annually, the organisation had no centralised way to monitor trends, evaluate treatment outcomes, or identify where injury risk was concentrated. Medical decisions were being made reactively, after athletes were already down, rather than from a position of data-driven foresight. This project builds a structured analytics solution on top of four years of Nexus Care injury data, covering 15,000 recorded injury events across five sports.

[Media Campaign Analysis](https://github.com/timiczn/Social-Media-Campaign-Performance-Analysis-)

I worked as a Data Analyst for a consumer brand to analyse a full year of their social media performance data. The goal was to move beyond surface-level metrics and uncover the specific patterns. By platform, content format, posting time, and campaign. Patterns that were actually driving results. The analysis fed into a fully interactive dashboard built in excel and a formal executive summary report delivered to senior leadership, containing 8 prioritised strategic recommendations.
